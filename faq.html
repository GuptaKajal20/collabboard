Questions, answered.

Everything about portfolios, collaboration openings, eligibility and getting started.

Is Collab Pro free?

Yes. Creating an account, discovering collaborations and building and publishing your portfolio are free. There are no paid plans right now.

Where do the collaboration openings come from?

Every morning Collab Pro searches the public web for brand collaboration openings: brand application forms, public posts and creator calls. We collect them in one feed and link to the original source.

Are the openings verified?

Not yet. Openings are found automatically and are not checked by a person. Always read the original post, and never pay a brand or agency to join a campaign.

Do you take a commission on deals?

No. You apply directly on the brand's own post or form. Collab Pro is not part of the deal and does not handle payments.

Do I need a minimum number of followers?

No. Collab Pro is for creators of every size. When an opening states a follower minimum, we show it so you can filter for openings that fit you.

What can I add to my portfolio?

A profile header with your photo, text with formatting, links, numbers like followers and views, image carousels, PDFs such as a media kit, social links, your rates, and a live preview of your Instagram, Facebook, YouTube or LinkedIn.

Who can see my portfolio?

Only what you publish is public. Your published portfolio can be opened by anyone with the link and may appear in search engines. Your account, saved and applied lists and drafts stay private.

Can I change my portfolio after publishing?

Yes. Edit anything, then click Publish again. Your link stays the same.

Which devices does it work on?

Collab Pro works in the browser on phones, tablets and computers. Log in with the same email and password on any device.

Is Collab Pro only for creators in India?

It's built with Indian creators in mind, and the collaboration feed focuses on India, but anyone can create an account and a portfolio.
