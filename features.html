Home/Features

Everything a creator needs, in one place.

Discovery, tracking and your portfolio, working together, with more tools on the way.

Discover collaborations

Daily collab discovery

Collab Pro searches public posts, brand application forms and creator calls every morning and collects them in one feed, so you stop hopping between apps, groups and pages.

Filters that fit you

Narrow the feed to your niche, see only paid or only barter work, and check which openings state a follower minimum you already meet.

Apply at the source

We don't sit in the middle. Tap Apply and you go directly to the brand's post or form, with no commission and no extra steps.

Save and track

Save interesting openings with a star, and when you come back after applying, Collab Pro asks if you applied so your Applied list stays accurate.

Build your portfolio

Drag-and-drop portfolio

Build with a profile header, text, links, numbers, files, social links, rates and a social preview. Type directly on the page and see every change instantly.

Your own public link

Publish your portfolio to a clean link like /p/yourname and send it in DMs, emails and pitches. Update it any time; the link stays the same.

Photos, PDFs and previews

Show your best shots in a carousel, attach a media kit as a PDF brands can read without leaving the page, and embed your Instagram, Facebook, YouTube or LinkedIn.

Looks right everywhere

Switch between desktop and mobile previews while you edit, then open a full-screen preview to check every detail.

Your workflow

One account, every device

Log in with your email on any phone or laptop and pick up exactly where you left off.
