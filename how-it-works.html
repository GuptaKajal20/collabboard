Home/How it works

From sign-up to your first pitch.

Five simple steps, all in your browser.

  1. 01

    Create your free account

    Sign up with your email, then tell us your niche, follower range, city and language.

  2. 02

    Discover collaborations

    Browse fresh openings every morning. Filter by niche, paid or barter, and follower requirements.

  3. 03

    Apply at the source

    Tap Apply to open the brand's own post or form. Star openings to come back to, and track what you've applied to.

  4. 04

    Build your portfolio

    Drag in modules for your bio, numbers, rates, photos, PDFs and social previews. Edit everything in place.

  5. 05

    Publish and share

    Publish to your own link and send it to brands in DMs, emails and pitches. Update it whenever you like.

Tips for better results

  • Set your follower range honestly. Filters then highlight openings that state a minimum you meet.
  • Star first, apply later. Save openings while you browse, then apply in one sitting.
  • Mark what you applied to. When you return after applying, Collab Pro asks. Keep that list accurate so you can follow up.
  • Put your numbers near the top. Brands look for followers, views and rates first.
  • Add a PDF media kit. Brands can read it inside your portfolio without downloading anything.
